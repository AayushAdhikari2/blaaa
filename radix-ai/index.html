How it works

One visit, from the door to the bill.

Every step is recorded once and shown to the people who need it, nothing is typed twice.

  1. 1

    Register

    Reception creates the visit and prints a slip with a token and a QR code. The patient can also book online first.

  2. 2

    Wait with information

    The patient sees their place and an estimated time on their phone. Optional SMS tells them when they are called.

  3. 3

    See the doctor

    The doctor records the visit, orders tests and prescribes. Allergy and interaction checks run before anything is sent.

  4. 4

    Reports, medicines, bill

    Lab uploads the report, pharmacy dispenses from the batch that expires first, and charges land on one bill.

What’s inside

The tools a clinic needs every day.

Eight modules that share one patient visit, built and tested together.

Queue & tokens

Per-doctor token numbers that never repeat, waiting-time estimates and a public board.

Consent-based messages

SMS when registered, called, or when the report is ready. Patients can stop messages at any time.

Lab reports

Upload a report to the visit, track turnaround time and let the patient open it with a private link.

Pharmacy safety

Allergy and drug-interaction checks, including earlier prescriptions, first-expiry-first-out stock and automatic billing.

Wards & beds

Admit, transfer and discharge with a live bed board and bed charges added to the bill.

Billing & claims

Itemised bills with QR, and insurance claims that must move through the steps in order.

Dashboard

Visits, average wait, consultation time, report turnaround, revenue and unpaid amounts, with CSV export.

Security built in

Role-based access, two-factor login, automatic logout and a tamper-evident audit log.

Privacy & safety

Careful with patient data.

Practical safeguards you can see and test, not badges.

Tamper-evident audit log

Every change is written to an append-only log chained with hashes. Personal details are masked in the log, and an admin can verify the chain at any time.

Is it ready for real patients?

It is in a pilot stage. We recommend a two-week pilot with paper kept as backup, and a legal review of the privacy notice and record-retention period before you go live.

Do patients have to install an app?

No. The patient page works in any phone browser, and staff can add RADIX AI to the home screen like an app.

How do SMS messages work?

Messages go out only when the patient consented at registration or booking. They cover registration, being called, “N patients ahead” and report ready. A patient can opt out at any time.

Does it work without internet?

No. A banner tells staff when the connection drops, but registering and saving need internet.

Which languages and time zone?

The interface is in English, and times use Asia/Kathmandu.

Are the medicine and interaction lists final?

No. The starting lists are samples. A pharmacist must review or replace them before real use, and admins can edit allergy terms in the app.
