Who we are

[Clinic name], [address], [phone / email]. Privacy contact: [name and email].

What we collect and why

  • To arrange and give you care: name, phone, age, reason for visit, allergies, test and prescription records, bills, and insurance details if you claim insurance.
  • To contact you about your visit (only if you agree): SMS text messages such as “you are registered”, “2 patients ahead of you”, “your report is ready”. Messages never contain your access code or the name of a test.
  • To keep the system safe: a log of which staff member changed which record. The log does not store your name or phone number.

Who can see your information

Only staff who need it for their job: reception sees registration and billing, your doctor sees your consultation and prescriptions, the lab sees test orders, the pharmacy sees prescriptions and allergies. Your reports are stored privately; the link to open one expires after 5 minutes. We do not sell your information. Our SMS provider ([provider name]) receives your phone number and the message text so it can deliver the message. [Add any other service providers and the country they operate in.]

How long we keep it

We keep patient details for [period, for example 3 years], then remove your name, phone, reason for visit, allergies and report files. Billing totals are kept in a form that does not identify you. [Check legal requirements for medical records.]

Your choices and rights

  • Stop SMS: open your status page and press “Stop SMS updates”, or tell reception.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion: use “Privacy request” on the patient status page, or ask reception. We check who you are first and answer within [30] days. We may have to keep medical records for a legal period; if so we will explain.
  • Complain: contact [name and email]. [Add the authority you can complain to.]

Security

Staff sign in with a password and an authenticator app, accounts are logged out after 15 minutes of no activity, access is limited by role, and changes are recorded in a tamper-evident log. No system is perfectly secure. If your information is ever exposed we will tell you [and the authorities if required].
